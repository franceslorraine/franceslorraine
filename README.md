## 🧠 How I Work

- **Start from the business context.** Having an ABM background means I look at numbers through a financial and operational lens first, ensuring technical projects directly address business goals.
- **Data quality is built in the query layer.** Cleaning, validation, and modeling happen at the source so that end-users trust every metric on the dashboard.
- **If it's done twice, automate it.** From sales incentive payout calculations to report generation, I leverage Python, SQL, and Google Apps Script to eliminate manual grunt work.
- **Code with purpose.** My computer science training allows me to design scalable databases and clean architecture, not just quick-fix spreadsheets.
